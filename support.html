Stillfield Support

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Stillfield is a night and long-exposure camera for iPhone focused on honest capture: enhance/AI off by default, and interval sessions that write files to disk.

Common questions

  • Permission: If the camera preview is blank, check Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera and ensure Stillfield is allowed.
  • Saves: After a session, check Photos for new images. Interval / overnight runs are designed to flush frames so a finished session leaves files on disk.
  • Enhance / AI: The default is off. Turn the in-app toggle on only when you want enhancement.
