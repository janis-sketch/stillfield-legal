Stillfield Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Stillfield (“the App”) is an on-device night and long-exposure camera for iPhone. This policy explains what data the App handles.

Data we process

  • Photos and capture sessions are created on your device. The App can save images to your Photos library using the system photo picker / save APIs.
  • Capture preferences (for example enhance/AI toggle state) stay on your device.
  • The App does not require an account. We do not operate our own servers for your photos.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the current App version.

Camera and Photos permissions

iOS may ask for Camera and Photos access so you can capture and save. Those permissions are controlled by Apple’s system settings. We do not receive copies of your photos on our servers.

Purchases

The App is a one-time paid App Store purchase. Apple processes payment. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions: janisscholzapple@gmail.com or the support page linked from the App Store listing.

Changes

We may update this policy when the App’s data practices change. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
